TRAVEL RESPONSIBLY

Safety Guidelines

Safety and responsible travel should always come first.

01. Verify Information

Always verify relevant information through trusted and approved channels.

02. Protect Privacy

Never share passwords, private contact details or sensitive personal information publicly.

03. Follow Guidelines

Follow institutional rules and seek guidance from responsible adults.

04. Emergency Support

In an emergency, contact local emergency services or a trusted adult.

This project is a frontend demonstration and does not arrange actual transportation.

WHO WE ARE

About COMMUTE POOL

COMMUTE POOL is a student-focused ride-sharing concept designed to explore smarter and more economical commuting.

This project demonstrates how technology can help users understand ride-sharing concepts through a simple website interface.

Our Vision

To explore convenient, economical and responsible transportation solutions through technology.
